How Does an Airbnb Cleaning Cost Calculator Work?

Short-term rentals often require cleaning between guest stays. The work may include changing bed linen, cleaning bathrooms, sanitizing surfaces, preparing the kitchen and checking that the property is ready for the next guest.

Property Size and Room Count

Larger properties may require more cleaning time. Bedrooms and bathrooms also affect the work involved, so use a rate that reflects the actual service you provide.

Laundry and Guest Turnover

Laundry costs can include washing and drying towels, bed linen and other supplied textiles. If you manage multiple turnovers, enter the number of cleanings you want to estimate.

Cleaning Supplies and Additional Services

Include cleaning products, consumables and other expenses. Optional services such as deep cleaning, window cleaning, restocking and dishwashing can increase the final quote.

Calculate Business Profit

Enter your expected labor, laundry, supplies and other costs. The calculator compares the service-based price with a cost-based price designed to meet your desired profit margin.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost?

The price depends on property size, location, cleaning requirements, laundry and additional services. Enter your own rates to calculate an estimate for your property.

What is Airbnb turnover cleaning?

Turnover cleaning is the preparation of a short-term rental between guest stays. It can include general cleaning, bathroom and kitchen cleaning, linen changes and restocking.

Should laundry be included in the cleaning fee?

That depends on your service agreement. You can include laundry in your base price or charge for it separately. Account for the actual cost of washing, drying and handling linen.

How do I price Airbnb cleaning as a business?

Estimate labor, supplies, laundry and overhead. Consider property size, condition and additional services, then set a price that covers your costs and desired profit.

Can I calculate prices in different currencies?

Yes. You can select USD, INR, GBP, EUR, CAD or AUD and enter prices for that currency. The calculator does not perform automatic currency conversion.

Can vacation rental hosts use this calculator?

Yes. Vacation rental hosts, property managers and cleaning companies can use it to prepare a cleaning budget or estimate.
