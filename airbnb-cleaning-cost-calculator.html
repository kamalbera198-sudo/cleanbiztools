How to Price Airbnb Cleaning Services

Setting a suitable vacation rental cleaning fee helps cleaning businesses cover their expenses and earn a profit. Airbnb turnover cleaning may involve changing linens, cleaning bathrooms, preparing kitchens, removing rubbish and checking the property between guests.

The right price depends on property size, bedroom and bathroom count, the condition of the rental, labor requirements, laundry, supplies and the number of turnovers. Additional tasks should be priced separately when they require extra time or materials.

1. Consider Property Size

Larger properties generally require more cleaning time. Use the property's square footage as one factor when estimating a base price. Adjust your rate to match the service level and your local market.

2. Calculate Your Operating Costs

Include staff wages, laundry, cleaning products, transport and other business expenses. Ignoring these costs can make a cleaning job appear profitable when the actual profit is much smaller.

3. Price Optional Services Separately

Deep cleaning, window cleaning, guest supply restocking and dishwashing can require extra work. Use the optional add-ons in this calculator to include these services when requested by a property owner or manager.

4. Review Your Profit Margin

A profit margin is the share of your selling price that remains after the included business costs. This calculator estimates a price based on your entered target margin and compares it with the base property estimate.

Airbnb Cleaning Price Formula

Estimated Total Price = Recommended Price per Turnover × Number of Turnovers.

Estimated Total Profit = Total Price − Estimated Business Costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate Airbnb cleaning costs?

Enter the property details, cleaning rate and operating expenses. Select any additional services, then calculate the estimated turnover price, total costs and profit.

What is an Airbnb turnover cleaning?

Turnover cleaning is the cleaning and preparation performed between guest stays. Tasks often include cleaning rooms and bathrooms, resetting the kitchen, changing linens and preparing the property for the next arrival.

Can I include deep cleaning and window cleaning?

Yes. Select the relevant add-on and enter its additional price per turnover. The calculator will include that amount in the estimate.

Can I include guest supplies restocking and dishwashing?

Yes. Both are optional services. Select the relevant checkbox and enter its price if you want it included in your cleaning quote.

Why do I need to enter labor and supply costs?

These costs help estimate how much the job may cost your business and how much profit could remain after the included expenses.

Can I use this calculator outside the United States?

Yes. Select an available currency for displaying your estimates. Currency selection changes the displayed currency symbol; it does not convert amounts between currencies.

Is this calculator free?

Yes. You can use the calculator without creating an account.
