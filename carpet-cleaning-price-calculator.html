    How to price carpet cleaning: per square foot vs per room

    Carpet cleaners usually quote in one of a few ways. Each has a place, and the right choice depends on the job.

    MethodWorks best forWatch out for
    Per square foot or metreLarge open areas, offices and other commercial carpetYou need to measure. Small jobs can come out too low, so set a minimum charge.
    Per roomHomes with standard room sizes and quick phone quotesVery large rooms can be underpriced unless you set a size limit per room.
    Per item or stepStairs, hallways, rugs and small spacesCustomers can lose track of many small lines. Keep the list short.
    Per hourHeavy restoration or unusual jobsOpen-ended totals worry customers. Give an estimate and a cap.

    Housecall Pro's 2026 pricing guide describes room and square-foot pricing as the best balance of speed and accuracy for most residential jobs, and says commercial work is more often priced by square foot or by hour. Part C of the calculator prices a job by the hour, and the costs box lets you check what a job really costs you per hour.

    What affects carpet cleaning prices

    Size of the job

    More carpet means more time, more water and more chemicals. Stairs and tight spaces take longer per square foot than open rooms.

    Soil and stains

    Heavy dirt, pet accidents and set-in stains need pre-treatment and extra passes. Many cleaners add a surcharge or price these separately.

    Carpet type

    Fibre, pile height and age change how the carpet should be cleaned and how long it takes to dry.

    Cleaning method

    Hot water extraction, low-moisture cleaning and bonnet cleaning use different equipment and take different amounts of time.

    Access and extras

    Furniture to move, floors up a flight of stairs, hard parking and long drives all add cost.

    Your local market

    Wages, fuel, insurance and competitors change from city to city and country to country.

    How much should I charge for carpet cleaning?

    Start with what the job costs you: labour, chemicals, fuel, equipment wear and a share of your overhead. Then decide the margin you need. A simple way to turn a cost into a price is:

    Price = job cost ÷ (1 − margin)

    Example (made-up numbers). A job costs you 60 in labour, supplies and fuel, and you want a 40% margin. The price is 60 ÷ 0.60 = 100.

    Then check that price against your local market. If it is far above what others charge, look at your costs or your method. If it is far below, you may be leaving money on the table.

    Quote tips that prevent arguments

    • Write what is included and what is not, such as furniture moving and stain treatment.
    • Ask about pets, heavy stains and stairs before you give a price.
    • Set a minimum job charge so small visits still pay for your time.
    • Take a deposit on larger jobs and say when the balance is due.
    • Say that the price is an estimate and may change if the job is different from what was described.

    Carpet cleaning price calculator: frequently asked questions

    How do I calculate a carpet cleaning price?

    Pick a pricing method, then work from your costs. For area pricing, multiply the carpet area by your rate per square foot or square metre. For room pricing, multiply the number of rooms by your price for each room. For hourly pricing, multiply the hours by your hourly rate and the number of cleaners. Add extras such as stain treatment, pet odor treatment and carpet protector, then apply your minimum charge, discount, travel fee and tax. This calculator does all of that as you type.

    How much do carpet cleaners charge per square foot?

    A 2026 Housecall Pro guide lists about $0.20 to $0.50 per square foot for residential carpet cleaning in the US and $0.10 to $0.30 for commercial work. Those are reference figures only. Your own rate should cover labour, supplies, fuel, equipment, insurance and profit.

    How much do carpet cleaners charge per room?

    The same guide lists about $25 to $75 per room for typical residential jobs. Thumbtack data updated in March 2026 puts the US national average for a whole job at roughly $147 to $288, with most people paying around $206. Prices differ a lot by city and country.

    Should I charge per square foot or per room?

    Per room is quick and easy for customers to understand, and it suits homes with standard room sizes. Per square foot is fairer for large open areas and commercial carpet, but you need to measure. Many cleaners price rooms and stairs by item and large open areas by area. This calculator lets you use both on one quote.

    What is a minimum job charge?

    It is the lowest amount you will accept for a visit. Small jobs still cost you travel time, setup and equipment wear, so a minimum charge stops a tiny job from losing money. Enter it in the calculator and it tops up any quote that falls below it.

    How should I charge for stains and pet odor?

    Most cleaners list these as add-ons so the base price stays clear. You can charge per stained spot, per room or as a flat fee. As a reference, the Housecall Pro guide lists stain removal at about $20 to $40 per room.

    Can I print the quote for my customer?

    Yes. Add your business name and the customer's name, then press Print quote. The printed page shows only the customer-facing quote. Your private cost and profit figures are left off.

    Yes, it is free and needs no sign-up. It gives estimates only. Final prices depend on the real job, your local costs and your own judgment.
