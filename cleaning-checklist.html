Move Out Cleaning: What to Clean Before You Hand Back the Keys

Move out cleaning goes deeper than a regular clean because the property is empty. You can reach inside cabinets, under where furniture stood, and behind appliances. Cleaning thoroughly helps avoid disputes with landlords or buyers about the condition of the property.

Start at the top of each room and finish with the floors, so dust and dirt fall onto surfaces you have not yet cleaned.

Follow product labels, never mix cleaning chemicals, and wear suitable protective equipment when required.

Move Out Cleaning Tips

  • Clean top to bottom, and finish with the floors in each room.
  • Start with the kitchen and bathrooms, which take the longest.
  • Take dated photos when you finish as a record of the condition.
  • Do not mix cleaning chemicals, and ventilate while you work.
  • Check the lease for tasks like carpet cleaning or oven cleaning.

Move Out Cleaning Checklist for Renters

If you rent, move out cleaning is often part of getting your security deposit back. Landlords usually compare the property with its condition when you moved in, so the cleaner you leave it, the fewer disagreements you are likely to have.

Keep your lease, your move-in photos and any inspection report close while you clean. If the lease names specific tasks, such as professional carpet cleaning, add them to your list.

Cleaning Supplies for Moving Out

Gather everything before you begin so you do not stop halfway through.

  • All-purpose cleaner and glass cleaner
  • Oven cleaner and a degreaser
  • Bathroom cleaner for tile and grout
  • Microfibre cloths, sponges and a scrub brush
  • Mop, bucket, broom and dustpan
  • Vacuum cleaner with attachments
  • Rubber gloves and trash bags
  • A step stool for high shelves and fixtures

Read every label, never mix chemicals, and ventilate while you clean.

Move Out Cleaning Order: A Simple Plan

Cleaning in the right order saves time and stops you from re-cleaning surfaces.

  1. Wait until all furniture and belongings are out of the property.
  2. Start with the kitchen and bathrooms, which take the longest.
  3. Clean top to bottom in every room: fixtures, walls, doors, then floors.
  4. Vacuum carpets and mop hard floors last, working towards the exit.
  5. Do the final walk-through, take photos and return the keys.

Move Out Cleaning Checklist FAQ

What should I clean before moving out?

Clean every room, including inside the oven, fridge, cabinets, closets and bathrooms, plus floors, baseboards, windows and light fixtures. Check your lease for any extra requirements.

Do I need to clean the oven and fridge when moving out?

These are commonly expected, but requirements vary. Read your lease or move-out instructions and ask your landlord if unsure.

How long does move out cleaning take?

It depends on the size and condition of the property and how many people clean. A checklist helps you plan the work and not miss areas.

Can professional cleaners use this checklist?

Yes. Cleaners can use it to agree the scope with the client and tick each task off during the job.

Should I clean before or after the movers leave?

After. Once furniture and boxes are out, you can reach every surface and floor, and nothing new will be dirtied.

What is the difference between move in and move out cleaning?

Move out cleaning is done by the person leaving and covers the whole empty property. Move in cleaning is done before the next occupant arrives, and often focuses on sanitizing kitchens and bathrooms.

How much does move out cleaning cost?

It depends on the size and condition of the property and local rates. Cleaning businesses can estimate it with the cleaning pricing calculator.
