Residential Cleaning Checklist

A residential cleaning checklist helps organize common household tasks. The right tasks depend on the property size, requested service, and client preferences.

  • Dust accessible surfaces and furniture.
  • Clean kitchen counters and exterior appliance surfaces.
  • Clean sinks, toilets, mirrors, and bathroom surfaces.
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs where appropriate.
  • Sweep and mop suitable hard floors.
  • Empty agreed-upon waste bins.
  • Check rooms against the client's instructions.

Follow product labels, avoid mixing cleaning chemicals, and use suitable protective equipment when required.

Commercial Cleaning Checklist

Commercial cleaning tasks may include offices, reception areas, meeting rooms, staff kitchens, and restrooms. Follow the building's access, security, hygiene, and safety procedures.

  • Clean and organize agreed reception and office areas.
  • Wipe suitable desks and shared touchpoints.
  • Clean meeting room tables and accessible surfaces.
  • Clean restrooms according to the service agreement.
  • Clean kitchen and break-room surfaces.
  • Empty designated waste and recycling bins.
  • Vacuum or mop floors according to surface requirements.
  • Check that the agreed tasks are complete before leaving.

Why Use a Cleaning Checklist?

1. Organize daily work

A checklist divides a cleaning job into manageable tasks and helps workers follow a consistent sequence.

2. Track completed tasks

Marking completed items makes it easier to review the job before leaving.

3. Improve communication

A shared checklist can help cleaning teams understand the scope of work and the client's agreed requirements.

4. Support business planning

Combine task checklists with pricing and profit estimates to plan services and understand your business costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cleaning checklist?

A cleaning checklist is a list of tasks used to organize a cleaning job and track which tasks have been completed.

Does this checklist save my progress automatically?

No. In this version, your checked tasks remain available while the page stays open. Reloading the page resets the checklist.
