House Cleaning Checklist, Room by Room

The checklist above covers the living room, kitchen, bathroom, bedrooms, entryway and a final inspection. Use it for your own home, a regular client, or a one-time visit. The right tasks depend on property size, the requested service, and client preferences.

  • Dust surfaces and furniture, top to bottom.
  • Clean kitchen counters, sink and appliance exteriors.
  • Scrub the toilet, shower or tub, sink and mirror.
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs, then mop hard floors last.
  • Empty agreed waste bins and replace liners.
  • Do a final walk-through against the client's instructions.

Follow product labels, never mix cleaning chemicals, and wear suitable protective equipment when required.

Office Cleaning Checklist for Commercial Cleaners

A commercial cleaning checklist covers shared spaces: reception, desks, meeting rooms, break rooms and restrooms. Follow the building's access, security, hygiene and safety procedures, and clean only what the service agreement includes.

  • Clean reception counters and agreed desk areas.
  • Wipe shared touchpoints such as door handles and switches.
  • Clean meeting room tables and straighten chairs.
  • Clean restrooms and restock supplies if included.
  • Clean break-room counters, sink and appliance exteriors.
  • Empty waste and recycling bins, then vacuum or mop floors.
  • Confirm all agreed tasks are done before leaving the site.

Daily, Weekly and Monthly Cleaning Checklist

Not every task needs doing on every visit. A simple split keeps a home or office tidy without wasting time. Adjust it to the property, how many people use it, and the service agreement.

Daily

  • Wipe kitchen counters and sink
  • Empty full bins
  • Sweep high-traffic floors
  • Wipe bathroom sink and toilet
  • Wipe shared touchpoints (offices)

Weekly

  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Mop hard floors
  • Dust furniture and shelves
  • Clean mirrors and glass
  • Change bed linen
  • Deep clean toilets and showers

Monthly

  • Wipe cabinet fronts and doors
  • Dust vents, baseboards and light fixtures
  • Clean inside microwave and fridge
  • Wash interior windows
  • Vacuum under and behind furniture

Cleaning Supplies and Equipment Checklist

Check your kit before every job so nothing is missing once you arrive.

  • All-purpose cleaner
  • Glass cleaner
  • Bathroom cleaner or disinfectant
  • Microfibre cloths
  • Sponges and scrub brushes
  • Mop and bucket
  • Broom and dustpan
  • Vacuum cleaner
  • Toilet brush
  • Extension duster
  • Rubber gloves
  • Trash bags

Using separate cloths for bathrooms and kitchens helps avoid spreading germs. Read every product label and never mix chemicals.

Cleaning Checklist FAQ

What should a cleaning checklist include?

A good checklist lists tasks room by room: dusting, surfaces, kitchen, bathrooms, floors, bins and a final inspection. For a business, also include client instructions, areas to skip and the supplies needed.

How often should a house be cleaned?

It depends on the household. Many people do quick daily tasks, a fuller clean weekly, and deeper jobs such as cabinet fronts and vents monthly. See the daily, weekly and monthly split on this page.

Can I print this cleaning checklist or save it as a PDF?

Yes. Choose a checklist and select Print or Save as PDF. In the print window you can pick a printer, or choose a PDF option if your device offers one.

Can I use this checklist for a cleaning business?

Yes. It helps organize residential and commercial jobs. Customize the tasks to match your services, customer instructions and workplace safety requirements.

What is the difference between a residential and a commercial cleaning checklist?

A residential checklist covers rooms in a home, such as bedrooms, kitchen and bathrooms. A commercial checklist covers offices and shared areas, and follows building access, security and hygiene rules.

Does this checklist save my progress?
