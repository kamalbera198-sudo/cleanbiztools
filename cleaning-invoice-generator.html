How to Create a Cleaning Invoice in 5 Steps

This free cleaning invoice generator is built for house cleaners, office and commercial cleaning companies, carpet cleaners, Airbnb turnover crews and janitorial businesses. You get a clean, printable invoice without accounting software or an account.

1. Your business

Add your name, contact details, address and tax number so the customer knows who to pay.

2. Customer

Enter the customer name and the address where the cleaning took place.

3. Services

Use Quick add for house, deep, office or carpet cleaning, then set quantity and rate.

4. Totals

Add a discount, tax rate and any amount already paid. The balance updates itself.

5. Print or PDF

Press Print / Save as PDF and choose Save as PDF in the print window.

What to Include on a Cleaning Service Invoice

A complete cleaning invoice is paid faster and causes fewer disputes. Make sure it shows:

  • A unique invoice number, the invoice date and a clear payment due date.
  • Your business name, contact details and tax ID if you charge tax.
  • The customer name and the service address.
  • Each cleaning job on its own line with quantity, rate and amount.
  • Subtotal, discount, tax, total and the balance still due.
  • How to pay: bank details, payment link or accepted methods.

How to Price the Lines on Your Invoice

Most cleaning businesses bill in one of three ways, and this tool handles all of them:

Flat rate

Enter quantity 1 and the agreed price, for example one deep cleaning at a fixed amount. Not sure what to charge? Try the Cleaning Price Calculator or send a cleaning quote first.

Hourly rate

Enter the hours as the quantity and your hourly rate as the rate. The Cleaning Hourly Rate Calculator helps you set a rate that covers labor, supplies and travel.

Per room, per area or add-ons

Add one row per room or extra, such as inside oven, fridge or windows. For rugs and upholstery, see the Carpet Cleaning Price Calculator and use its result as a line on a carpet cleaning invoice.

Example invoice lines
Deep Cleaning, 1 × 180.00 = 180.00
Carpet Cleaning (2 rooms), 2 × 55.00 = 110.00
Inside Oven, 1 × 25.00 = 25.00

Tips to Get Paid Faster

  • Send the invoice the same day the job is finished, while the result is fresh.
  • Use a short due date such as 7 days, and say it in the notes.
  • Offer more than one payment method and put the details on the invoice.
  • Track your margins with the Cleaning Profit Calculator so discounts do not eat your profit.
  • Starting out? The Cleaning Startup Cost Calculator shows what to budget before your first invoice.

Invoice, Quote and Receipt: What Is the Difference?

A quote is the price you offer before the job. An invoice is the bill you send after the work. A receipt confirms payment. To mark an invoice as settled, set the status to Paid and the amount paid fills in automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this cleaning invoice generator free?

Yes. You can create, update, print and save invoices as PDFs for free, without creating an account.

Can I invoice different cleaning services?

Yes. Add as many rows as you need for house cleaning, deep cleaning, office cleaning, carpet cleaning and other services.

Which currencies can I use?

USD, INR, GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD, NZD, AED, PKR, BDT and ZAR. The currency only changes how amounts are shown. It does not convert exchange rates.

How do I save my invoice as a PDF?

Click Print / Save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the destination in your browser's print dialog. Options vary by browser and device.

Does the tool calculate taxes automatically?

It applies the percentage you enter, after any discount. It does not decide which taxes apply to your business, so check your local rules.

Does it store my customer information?

No. Nothing is sent to a server or saved in a database. The details stay on the page until you clear the form or close the tab, so avoid using shared devices for confidential data.
