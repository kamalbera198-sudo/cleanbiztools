How Does a Cleaning Pricing Calculator Work?

A cleaning pricing calculator helps cleaning business owners estimate what to charge for residential or commercial cleaning jobs. The price can depend on property size, service type, labor, supplies, travel and the profit margin you want to achieve.

Step 1: Calculate the Base Price

Base Price = Property Size ÷ 1,000 × Base Rate × Service Factor

Step 2: Calculate Estimated Costs

Estimated Costs = Labor Cost + Supplies and Other Costs

Step 3: Estimate the Suggested Price

Cost-Based Price = Estimated Costs ÷ (1 − Profit Margin)

This calculator compares the base estimate with the price needed to cover the entered costs and target profit margin, then displays the higher amount as the suggested price.

Cleaning Pricing Tips for Small Businesses

  • Estimate how much time each cleaning job will take.
  • Include labor, supplies, travel and other relevant expenses.
  • Deep cleaning may require more time than standard cleaning.
  • Review local competitor rates before setting your final price.
  • Consider the property's condition and the customer's requirements.
  • Recalculate prices when your business expenses change.
  • Review your actual costs and profit after completing each job.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I charge for house cleaning?

Your price depends on property size, cleaning type, labor, expenses, local demand and your business goals. Use this calculator as a starting estimate rather than a fixed market price.

How do I calculate a cleaning price?

Estimate the base price using the property size, base rate and cleaning type. Add labor and supply costs, then check whether the suggested price supports your target profit margin.

Should deep cleaning cost more than standard cleaning?

Deep cleaning often involves more detailed work and may take longer than standard cleaning. Adjust the service type and costs to reflect the actual work required.

How do I estimate move-out cleaning costs?

Consider the property's size and condition, the time required, supplies and the cleaning tasks requested. The final price may vary by location and job requirements.

Can I use this calculator for office cleaning?

Yes. Select Office Cleaning and adjust the base rate and expenses to suit the job. Consider the office size, cleaning frequency and requested services.

What is a target profit margin?

A target profit margin is the percentage of the final selling price you aim to retain as profit after accounting for the costs included in your calculation.

Can I use this calculator outside the United States?

Yes. You can select USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, AUD or INR. Remember that changing the currency symbol does not automatically convert exchange rates. Enter prices and costs appropriate for your chosen currency and local market.
