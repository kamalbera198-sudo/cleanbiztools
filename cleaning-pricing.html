How Does a Cleaning Pricing Calculator Work?

A cleaning pricing calculator helps cleaning business owners estimate what to charge for residential or commercial cleaning jobs. The price can depend on home size, cleaning type, labor, supplies, travel, and the profit margin you want to achieve.

Suggested price formula

Base Price = Home Size ÷ 1,000 × Base Rate × Service Factor

Suggested Price = Total Estimated Costs ÷ (1 − Profit Margin)

The calculator uses the service factor to adjust the base estimate for different cleaning types. Actual costs and prices vary by location.

Cleaning Pricing Tips

  • Estimate the time required for each cleaning job.
  • Include labor, supplies, travel, and other relevant costs.
  • Deep cleaning may require more time than standard cleaning.
  • Review local prices before setting your final rate.
  • Recalculate prices when your business expenses change.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I charge for house cleaning?

Your price depends on home size, service type, labor, expenses, local demand, and your business goals. Use the calculator as a starting estimate rather than a fixed market rate.

Should deep cleaning cost more?

Deep cleaning often involves more detailed work and time, so businesses may charge more than they charge for standard cleaning.

What is a target profit margin?

It is the percentage of the final price you aim to retain as profit after the costs entered into the calculator.

Can I use this calculator for office cleaning?

Yes. Select Office Cleaning and adjust the base rate and expenses to suit the job.
