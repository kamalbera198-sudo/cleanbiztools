How to Create a Cleaning Quote

A cleaning quote tells a customer what a job will cost before work begins. Enter your business and customer details, choose the cleaning type and property size, add extra services, discount and tax, then print or save the quote as a PDF. The result updates as you type.

What should a cleaning quote include?

  • Your business name, quote number and quote date.
  • Customer contact information and property address.
  • The type of cleaning service and how often it repeats.
  • Property size, your rate, and any extra services.
  • Discount, tax, total price and how long the quote is valid.
  • Service notes and payment terms.

Quote vs estimate

An estimate is an approximate price that may change after the job is seen. A quote is a firmer price for a defined scope of work, normally valid for a set number of days. Use the notes field to state exactly what is included.

How to write a cleaning quote step by step

  1. Confirm the scope: which rooms, how large, and which tasks are included or excluded.
  2. Choose the cleaning type and how often the service repeats.
  3. Enter the property size and your base rate. The calculator applies the cleaning type multiplier.
  4. Add extra bathrooms and extra services such as inside the oven, inside the fridge, or interior windows.
  5. Apply any discount, minimum charge and tax.
  6. Set how long the quote is valid and your payment terms.
  7. Print or save the quote as a PDF and send it with a short message.

Residential vs commercial cleaning quotes

Residential: price mainly by home size, number of bathrooms, pets, current condition and access. A first visit often takes longer, so many cleaners quote the first clean as a deep clean and later visits as standard cleans.

Commercial: price by floor area, number of restrooms, visit schedule (often after hours), who supplies consumables, and contract length. Commercial work is usually recurring, so state the frequency and any minimum charge clearly.

What to write in the notes

  • Tasks that are included, and tasks that are not (for example inside cabinets or heavy mold removal).
  • Who provides supplies and equipment.
  • Access arrangements such as keys, entry codes or pets on site.
  • Your cancellation or rescheduling terms.

Sample message to send with the quote

"Hi [Name], thank you for contacting [Business]. Attached is your cleaning quote for [address], valid until [date]. It includes the tasks listed in the notes. Reply to this message to confirm a date, or let me know if you would like anything added or removed."

Common cleaning quote mistakes

  • Not stating exactly what is included, which leads to disputes later.
  • Leaving out travel, supplies or equipment costs. Check your margin with the Profit Calculator.
  • Forgetting a validity date, so an old price can be requested months later.
  • Quoting a first-time clean at the standard rate when it takes much longer.
  • Forgetting tax where it applies.

Tips to win more cleaning jobs

  • Send the quote quickly, ideally the same day you get the request.
  • Itemize the price so the customer sees what they are paying for.
  • Offer a recurring-service discount for weekly or monthly customers.
  • Follow up politely after two or three days if you have not heard back.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this cleaning quote calculator free?

Yes. You can use this calculator to prepare a cleaning quote for free.

Can I use it for deep cleaning?

Yes. Select Deep Cleaning from the service menu. It applies a 1.5 multiplier to your base rate. You can change the base rate to match your pricing.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Select USD, INR, GBP, EUR, CAD, or AUD. Currency selection changes the displayed symbol, not the exchange rate or the entered prices.

Can I save the quote as a PDF?

Choose Print / Save PDF. Only the quote is printed. In the print dialog, select Save as PDF.

Is the calculated price guaranteed?

No. It is based on the values you enter. Confirm the scope of work and final price with your customer.

What is the difference between a quote and an estimate?

An estimate is an approximate price that may change. A quote is a more fixed price for a defined scope of work, usually valid for a set number of days.

How do I price a cleaning job?

Start from your labor, supplies and travel costs, add your target profit, then enter your rate per 100 sq ft. The Pricing Calculator and Hourly Rate Calculator can help.

Is my customer information saved?

No. The details you enter stay in your browser and are not sent to or stored on a server.

What should I write in the notes of a cleaning quote?

List the tasks that are included and excluded, who provides supplies, access arrangements, and your cancellation terms.

How long should a cleaning quote be valid?

Many cleaning businesses use 7 to 30 days. Choose a period that reflects how quickly your costs and schedule change, and enter it in the Valid For field.

Should I charge more for a first-time deep clean?

Many cleaners do, because the first visit usually takes longer. Quote the first visit as Deep Cleaning and later visits as Standard Cleaning.
