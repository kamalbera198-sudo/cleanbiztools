How to Create a Cleaning Quote

A cleaning quote helps a residential or commercial cleaning business estimate the cost of a job before work begins. Enter the property size, select the cleaning service, add extra services, and apply a discount if needed. The calculator will display an estimated total.

What should a cleaning quote include?

  • Customer contact information and property address.
  • The type of cleaning service requested.
  • Property size and your base rate.
  • Additional services and any extra charges.
  • Discounts, estimated total, and service notes.

Who can use this calculator?

Independent cleaners, residential cleaning businesses, office cleaners, and cleaning service professionals can use this free tool to prepare preliminary estimates. Review the final quote before sending it to a customer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this cleaning quote calculator free?

Yes. You can use this calculator to prepare a basic cleaning estimate for free.

Can I use it for deep cleaning?

Yes. Select Deep Cleaning from the service menu. You can also change the rate to match your pricing.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Select USD, INR, GBP, EUR, CAD, or AUD. Currency selection changes the displayed symbol, not the exchange rate or the entered prices.

Can I save the quote as a PDF?

Choose Print / Save PDF. If your browser supports printing, select Save as PDF in the print dialog.

Is the calculated price guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate based on the values you enter. Confirm the scope of work and final price with your customer.
