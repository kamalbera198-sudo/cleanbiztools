How to Price Airbnb Cleaning Services

Setting a suitable vacation rental cleaning fee helps cleaning businesses cover their expenses and earn a profit. Airbnb turnover cleaning may involve changing linens, cleaning bathrooms, preparing kitchens, removing rubbish and checking the property between guests.

The right price depends on property size, bedroom and bathroom count, the condition of the rental, labor requirements, laundry, supplies and the number of turnovers. Additional tasks should be priced separately when they require extra time or materials.

1. Consider Property Size, Bedrooms and Bathrooms

Larger properties generally require more cleaning time. The calculator multiplies the square footage by your rate, then adds a charge for each bedroom and bathroom. Bathrooms usually take longer, so many cleaners charge more for them.

2. Calculate Your Operating Costs

Include staff wages, laundry, cleaning products, transport and other business expenses. Ignoring these costs can make a cleaning job appear profitable when the actual profit is much smaller.

3. Price Optional Services Separately

Deep cleaning, window cleaning, guest supply restocking and dishwashing can require extra work. Use the optional add-ons in this calculator to include these services when requested by a property owner or manager.

4. Review Your Profit Margin

A profit margin is the share of your selling price that remains after the included business costs. The calculator works out a size-based price and a cost-based price from your target margin, then uses the higher of the two so the job is not underpriced. Add-ons are added afterwards.

Airbnb Cleaning Price Formula

Size-based price = (Square feet × Rate + Bedrooms × Bedroom charge + Bathrooms × Bathroom charge) × Condition factor.

Cost-based price = Business costs per turnover ÷ (1 − Profit margin).

Price per turnover = Higher of the two prices + Selected add-ons. Total price = Price per turnover × Number of turnovers.

The results are planning estimates. Check local cleaning rates and confirm the requested tasks with the property owner before preparing a final quote.

Worked Example Using the Example Numbers

With the numbers already in the calculator (900 square feet at a rate of 0.10, 2 bedrooms at 8 each, 1 bathroom at 12 and standard condition), the size-based price is 90 + 16 + 12 = 118. Costs of 45 + 15 + 10 + 8 = 78 and a 30% margin give a cost-based price of about 111.43. The calculator uses the higher figure, so the price is 118 per turnover. For 4 turnovers that is a total of 472, costs of 312 and an estimated profit of 160. Ticking the deep cleaning add-on (40) raises the price to 158 per turnover. These are example numbers, not real market prices.

Airbnb Cleaning Fee vs Cleaning Service Price

Hosts often add a cleaning fee to the guest's booking, while a cleaner or cleaning company charges the host a service price for each turnover. This calculator helps cleaners work out the service price they should charge the host, including laundry, supplies, labor and profit.

Hosts can also use the result to check whether their cleaning fee covers what they pay for turnovers. Fees and rules vary by listing and platform, so check your own listing settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate Airbnb cleaning costs?

Enter the property size, bedrooms, bathrooms, your cleaning rates and your costs for labor, laundry, supplies and overhead. Add any optional services, then calculate the turnover price and multiply it by the number of turnovers.

What is an Airbnb turnover cleaning?

Turnover cleaning is the cleaning and preparation done between guest stays. Tasks often include cleaning rooms and bathrooms, resetting the kitchen, changing linens and preparing the property for the next arrival.

What are Airbnb cleaning add-ons?

Optional add-ons can include deep cleaning, window cleaning, guest supplies restocking and dishwashing. Tick a service and enter its extra price per turnover, and the calculator adds it to the estimate.

Can I calculate cleaning business profit?

Yes. Enter your labor, laundry, supplies and overhead costs per turnover. The calculator shows total costs and the estimated profit based on the price it calculates.

Why do I need to enter labor and supply costs?

These costs show how much the job may cost your business and how much profit could remain after the included expenses.

How much should I charge for Airbnb cleaning?

Add up your labor, laundry, supplies and overhead, add the profit margin you want, and compare the result with a size-based price and your local market. The calculator does both comparisons and uses the higher price before adding any extras.

What is the difference between an Airbnb cleaning fee and a cleaning service price?

A cleaning fee is what a host adds to the guest's booking. A cleaning service price is what a cleaner or cleaning company charges the host. This calculator estimates the cleaning service price.

How long does an Airbnb turnover take?

It depends on the size of the property, the number of bathrooms, the amount of laundry and the condition it is left in. Enter the labor cost for the time you expect to spend.

Should laundry be included in the Airbnb cleaning price?

Many cleaners treat laundry as a separate cost because washing and drying linen takes time and materials. Enter your laundry cost per turnover so it is covered in the price.

What does the profit margin mean in this calculator?

The profit margin is the share of the final price that is profit after your business costs. A 30 percent margin means 30 percent of the customer price is profit, not 30 percent added on top of your costs.

Can I use this calculator outside the United States?

Yes. Select an available currency for displaying your estimates. Currency selection changes the displayed symbol; it does not convert amounts between currencies.

Is this calculator free?

Yes. The CleanBizTools Airbnb cleaning cost calculator is free to use and does not require an account.
