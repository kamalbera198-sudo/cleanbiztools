How to Calculate Move Out Cleaning Costs

Move out cleaning prices depend on property size, cleaning time, the number of rooms and the level of dirt. Additional services, such as oven or carpet cleaning, may increase the final quote.

1. Enter the Property Size

Enter the property's approximate floor area in square feet. Use a consistent measurement when comparing properties.

2. Add Bedrooms and Bathrooms

Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, along with the charge you apply per room. Bathrooms usually take more time than other rooms, so many cleaners charge more for them.

3. Choose the Cleaning Condition

Light cleaning is suitable for well-maintained properties. Medium cleaning covers normal move-out work. Heavy cleaning accounts for properties that require additional time and effort.

4. Add Extra Services

Select any extra services requested by the customer and enter the relevant charges. Extra services are added on top of the base price.

5. Review Costs and Profit

Enter estimated labor, supplies and other business expenses. The calculator compares a size-based price with a cost-based price that covers your expenses and desired profit margin, then uses the higher of the two so the job is not underpriced. Extra services are added afterwards.

What Affects Move Out Cleaning Prices?

There is no single price for a move out clean. The final cost depends on several factors, and this calculator lets you enter your own rates for each one.

  • Property size: larger homes take more time and supplies.
  • Bedrooms and bathrooms: bathrooms and kitchens usually need the most work.
  • Condition: a well-kept property is faster to clean than one with built-up grime.
  • Extras: oven, refrigerator, window and carpet cleaning are often charged separately.
  • Labor, supplies and overhead: your own costs set the minimum price you can accept.
  • Location: local wages and market rates change what customers expect to pay.

Move Out Cleaning Cost Calculator FAQs

How much does move out cleaning cost?

The cost varies with property size, location, condition, cleaning requirements and additional services. Enter your local rates to generate an estimate.

What is included in move out cleaning?

Typical services include cleaning floors, surfaces, kitchens, bathrooms and accessible areas. Confirm the exact tasks with your cleaning provider or customer.

Is move out cleaning the same as end of tenancy cleaning?

These terms often describe similar cleaning services when someone leaves a rental property. Requirements differ by location and rental agreement.

How do cleaning businesses set a profitable price?

Calculate labor, supplies and overhead, then account for the profit margin you want to achieve. Compare your estimate with local competitors and the scope of work.

Can I use this calculator outside the United States?

Yes. You can select several currencies and enter rates and costs appropriate for your local market. The calculator does not convert currencies automatically.

Can tenants use this calculator?

Yes. Tenants, landlords, property managers and cleaning businesses can use it to prepare an initial budget or quote.

How much does move out cleaning cost per square foot?

Price per square foot depends on your local rates, the condition of the property and the services included. Enter your own rate per square foot in the calculator, and the result shows the price per square foot for the whole job, including extras.

How much does it cost to clean a 2 bedroom apartment for move out?

The price depends on the apartment's size, condition, location and extra services. Enter 2 bedrooms, the number of bathrooms and your local rates in the calculator to estimate a price for that apartment.

How much should I charge for a move out cleaning job?

Add up your labor, supplies and overhead, add the profit margin you want, and compare the result with a size-based price and your local market. The calculator does both comparisons and uses the higher price.

Do oven and carpet cleaning cost extra for move out cleaning?

They are often charged as extras. Tick the services the customer wants and enter your own charges, and the calculator adds them on top of the base price.

How much does end of tenancy cleaning cost?

The price depends on the size and condition of the property, your location, the services included and who does the work. Enter your own local rates in the calculator to estimate a price for end of tenancy cleaning.

How much does it cost to clean a 3 bedroom house for move out?

The cost depends on the size of the house, its condition, your location and any extras. Enter 3 bedrooms, the number of bathrooms, the square footage and your local rates in the calculator to estimate a price for that house.

How much does it cost to clean a 1 bedroom apartment for move out?

A 1 bedroom apartment is usually quicker to clean than a house, but the price still depends on size, condition, location and extras. Enter 1 bedroom, your bathroom count and your local rates to get an estimate.

How long does move out cleaning take?

It depends on the size and condition of the property and how many people are cleaning. Heavily soiled kitchens and bathrooms, and extras such as ovens and carpets, add time. Enter the labor cost for the time you expect the job to take.

Who pays for move out cleaning, the tenant or the landlord?

It depends on the lease and local rules. Many leases ask tenants to leave the property clean, but requirements differ, so check your rental agreement or ask your landlord or property manager.

Should I hire a professional move out cleaner or clean it myself?

Cleaning it yourself saves money but takes time and effort. A professional service can be faster and follow a set checklist. Use the calculator to compare a professional estimate with your own time and supply costs.

What is a move out cleaning checklist?

It is a room-by-room list of cleaning tasks for a property being vacated, such as kitchens, bathrooms, floors, doors and a final inspection. Use a printable checklist alongside your quote so everyone knows what is included.

How do I write a move out cleaning quote?

List the property details, the tasks included, any extras and the total price. Use this calculator to work out the price, then put the result and the agreed scope of work into a written quote.

What does the profit margin mean in this calculator?

The profit margin is the share of the final price that is profit after your business costs. A 30 percent margin means 30 percent of the customer price is profit, not 30 percent added on top of your costs.

Why does the calculator show a size-based or cost-based price?

The calculator works out one price from the size of the property and another from your costs and profit margin, then uses the higher one so the job is not underpriced. The Pricing Basis box shows which one was used.
