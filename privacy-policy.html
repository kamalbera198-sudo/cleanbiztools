Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit CleanBizTools.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Welcome to CleanBizTools. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website and use our online tools. Please read it carefully.

Important: This page is a general privacy policy template. Before publishing, make sure it accurately describes the technologies, services and data practices actually used by your website.

1. Information We Collect

CleanBizTools is designed to provide online calculators and informational resources. The information handled by the website depends on the features you use.

Information may include:

  • Information you provide: Information you choose to submit through a contact form or other interactive feature, if available.
  • Calculator inputs: Numbers and other values you enter into calculators to obtain results.
  • Technical information: Certain technical details, such as browser type, device information or server logs, may be processed by the hosting provider or other services.

Do not enter passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information into our calculators.

2. How Calculator Data Is Used

Calculator inputs are used to generate the results you request. Some tools may perform calculations directly in your browser. Whether information is stored or transmitted depends on the implementation of each tool and any third-party services used.

Do not assume that information is stored only on your device unless the specific tool has been verified to work that way.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that websites or third-party services may store in your browser. They can be used for functions such as remembering preferences, measuring website usage or supporting advertising.

CleanBizTools may use cookies or similar technologies if features requiring them are added or enabled. This policy should be updated to identify those technologies and explain their purposes when applicable.

Website analytics services, if used, may collect information about visits, page views, browser characteristics and interactions. This information may help us understand how visitors use the website and improve its features.

We will update this policy if we introduce analytics services and describe their relevant data practices.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

CleanBizTools may use third-party services in the future, including advertising providers. Such providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

If advertising is introduced, this policy should identify the applicable providers and explain how visitors can find their privacy information and available choices.

We do not claim that any particular advertising or analytics service is currently active unless it has actually been installed and verified.

6. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to other websites or services. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third parties. Please review their privacy policies before sharing personal information with them.

7. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate practices when handling information. However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended to provide general cleaning business information and tools. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through a feature on our website, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and the services used by this website, you may have rights or choices relating to your personal information. These may include requesting access, correction or deletion of information you have provided.

Applicable rights vary by jurisdiction. Requests should be handled according to the laws that apply to the website and the individual.
